## Data source and data-quality decision

The file `data/rwanda_national_park_visits_2005_2024.csv` contains 60 real park-year observations transcribed from NISR Statistical Yearbook 2025, Table 9.1. The source credits RDB. The total is calculated as the sum of the three park values in each year.

The official aggregate dataset does **not** meet the lecturer's “few hundred records or more” target on its own. A blank survey template is supplied at `data/anonymous_visitor_survey_template.csv`. Before collecting responses, obtain the required lecturer/institutional permission and any park/operator permission. Collect at least 200 anonymous responses if approved. Do not collect names, phone numbers, passport numbers, email addresses or other direct identifiers. Do not invent survey responses.
